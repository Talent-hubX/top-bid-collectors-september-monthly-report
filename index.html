Active Creative Styles — Testing in October

Five formats tested this month — GTA 6 / Version 1, GTA 6 / Version 2, Money, Viral Sound Effects, and Nuke Animation.

01

GTA 6 / Version 1

The pull is staged as a city billboard, then cut to a GTA-style street with the MISSION PASSED / RESPECT + stamp over the scene.

Why test itThe mission-passed stamp is a screen people already stop for. Putting the hit on the billboard ties the pull to a game frame before the viewer has to read the card.

Test video

02

GTA 6 / Version 2

The hit sits on the car seat. The cut is the mission-passed stamp, then a hand coming through the window for the card.

Why test itSame game frame as Version 1, different beat. The seat hold and the reach make the pull feel like the reward of the mission, not a billboard.

Test video

03

Money

The card is held in frame while bills fly across it and a green line spikes through the shot.

Why test itCash and a rising line make the hit readable without a caption. The graph break is the hook. The card is the proof.

Test video

04

Viral Sound Effects

Clean hold on the card, no overlay, cut to a viral sound effect on the reveal.

Why test itThe card stays fully readable. The sound does the hook, so the pull reads as a reaction instead of a catalog shot.

Test video

05

Nuke Animation

The hold blows out under a mushroom-cloud graphic and a hand-drawn NUKE stamp.

Why test itThe word and the cloud mark the pull as the hit of the break in one frame, before the viewer has to read the card.

Test video

04

Next Steps

Where the accounts stand as September closes, and what carries into October.

01

Scale the consistent winners

Fire Overlay (508 avg. eng., 8,436 avg. views, 12 posts) leads both rankings. Cat Meme (5,896 views) and Tunnel AI (5,052) are the next reach formats. These take priority in October's posting mix. Simple stays the volume baseline: 341 posts, 125 avg. eng., 4,624 avg. views.

02

Retire the bottom three

FIFA Card AI (1,340 avg. views, 19 avg. eng.), Countdown Reveal (2,381 / 35), and Fast Reveal (2,545 / 50) close September at the bottom of both rankings. No further production on any of the three in October.

03

Card Shop/Breaks carried the month

Card Shop/Breaks views went from 129,426 to 309,765 (↑ 149%) and engagement from 2,308 to 5,162 (↑ 135.6%). Collectors still grew (views ↑ 18.3%, engagement ↑ 5.6%). Sports was flat to down (views ↓ 1%, engagement ↓ 1.9%). Followers closed at 39K, ↑ 6.1%, and video output beat the target at 291/252.

Next Month's Creative Strategy — October

September's read: production exceeded target (291 videos vs. the 252/mo goal). Fire Overlay led both rankings (508 avg. eng., 8,436 avg. views on 12 posts). Card Shop/Breaks carried the month (views 129,426 to 309,765, engagement 2,308 to 5,162).

✂️

Retire the bottom three

FIFA Card AI (1,340 avg. views), Countdown Reveal (2,381), and Fast Reveal (2,545) generate the least reach of any label this month. No further production on any of the three.

🧪

New hypothesis: industry news, plus the new formats

Tests start on industry-news posts to see if this content pulls new audiences and lifts engagement. Alongside that, five new video formats go into the test pool: GTA 6 / Version 1, GTA 6 / Version 2, Money, Viral Sound Effects, and Nuke Animation.

➕

+100 Simple videos

October’s goal is to produce 100 additional Simple-format videos. We aim to increase total views by publishing more content. At the same time, we’ll continue testing new formats.

📊

KPIs for October

Card Shop/Breaks holding the September jump is the headline target (309,765 views, 5,162 engagement). Secondary: Sports stops the slide (views down 1%, engagement down 1.9%), Fire Overlay stays the lead label on a fresh sample, and followers continue to grow past 39K.
